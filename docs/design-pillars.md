# Forgeborne design pillars

Director brief, confirmed 2026-10-31: a studio-quality original merger of modded
Minecraft, StarCraft, Diablo, Path of Exile 2, Borderlands and BioShock. The full
vision remains in scope. These are references for strengths and player experience;
all shipped world, names, designs, code, art and audio must be Forgeborne originals.

| Pillar | Intended Forgeborne experience | Evidence that must eventually pass |
|---|---|---|
| Gather, craft, build and automate | The player changes a persistent voxel place, makes useful structures and progresses through mechanical/electrical/digital/arcane automation | Gather/build feedback is legible; costs/effects matter under pressure; edits and production survive saves; each tier works as a complete player flow |
| Real-time command | FPS participation and a true 3D overhead command view share one battle and economy; doctrine squads make spatial expansion meaningful | Switch views without a second state owner; select/deploy/rally/cancel clearly; squads obey doctrine/cover and remain readable in the real renderer |
| Combat and build progression | ARPG loot, active skills, passives and synergies change how an encounter is played, with deliberate choices and readable danger | Distinct tested builds plus player-visible feedback, useful loot decisions and encounter balance; graph size alone is not acceptance |
| Weapon variety and feel | Original modular weapon families combine usable mechanics, recoil/impact/audio identity, procedural variety and personality | Weapons feel different in play, advertise their function, hit what the player aims at and integrate with loot/save/load |
| Atmosphere and systemic world | An authored industrial sci-fi place tells its story through space and consequences; powers/elements interact consistently with the voxel world | Composition, lighting, original audio and environmental interactions communicate the same place; atmosphere does not hide gameplay |
| Studio craft | Cohesive final-quality 3D graphics, animation, effects, sound, input, UI, reliability and performance | A native review build, real device flows, sustained hardware measurements and Rob's quality/fun review |

The sequence remains gather → build → survive first. Establish the first polished
encounter before multiplying content, graph nodes or automation tiers; this is a
dependency order, not a reduction of the vision. Single-player comes first,
multiplayer stays deferred, and 2D/turn-based substitutes remain out of scope.

The production reference's discipline ownership and dependency/evidence gates
inform execution. Its proposed agent roles do not guarantee creative cohesion,
correctness, linear coordination cost or a production timeline. Direct rendered,
heard and played evidence remains necessary.
