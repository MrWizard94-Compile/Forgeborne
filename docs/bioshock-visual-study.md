# BioShock visual study — 2026-11-02

Rob requested actual BioShock references after noting the texture improvement
and the remaining quality gap. These are observational art-direction notes,
not a claim of matching that quality. The equally important six-influence game
brief remains unchanged. Images were viewed remotely; no third-party image,
model, texture, sign, character or level was added to Forgeborne's assets.

## Viewed references

- [2K's BioShock page](https://store.2k.com/game/buy-bioshock) and
  [published lobby screenshot](https://images.ctfassets.net/wn7ipiv9ue5v/ccqbJaCjeCqNkdBupnnUP/24254915f3366573befed39881d7f193/The_City_in_the_Sky__4_.jpg).
  The image itself depicts the Rapture lobby; its CDN filename is not a reliable
  scene label. Observed: stairs/balconies create connected levels; curved railings,
  inset wall panels, fine metalwork and thick trims express construction and
  human scale. Individual lights frame signs and doorways. Cracked stone, painted
  surfaces and metal have distinct forms and reflectance. Wear clusters at joints
  and damaged areas. A focal sign has supporting detail and quieter surrounding
  surfaces rather than uniform detail everywhere.
- [Nintendo's BioShock 2 Remastered page](https://www.nintendo.com/pt-br/store/products/bioshock-2-remastered-switch/) and
  [published Adonis lobby screenshot](https://assets.nintendo.com/image/upload/ar_16%3A9%2Cb_auto%3Aborder%2Cc_lpad/b_white/f_auto/q_auto/dpr_1.5/ncom/software/switch/70010000026231/d2ffbea4692cefd8e8f4fa32aaef407e6317bd704b5b6769fae033ff827b18ad).
  Observed: the curved stair, planter, vegetation and damaged railing break the
  room's symmetry. Wet floor reflections and drips link surfaces to the setting.
  Dark upper volumes retain depth while local cyan and magenta light separate
  specific features. Plants and growth have continuous branching/tapering forms.
  The damaged place suggests prior use and a subsequent event.

These observations are interpretations of the pictured composition. No claim
is made about the games' shaders, triangle budgets or production methods.

## Consequences for the next original Forgeborne art pass

1. Give the exchange a believable purpose and history through original service
   fittings, readable entrances, operator stations, fasteners, cable routes,
   maintenance access and a few deliberately damaged assemblies.
2. Replace broad featureless planes and repeated silhouettes with modeled trim,
   panel depth, wall/floor transitions and connected architectural volumes.
   Preserve useful traversal space and test contact at those exact surfaces.
3. Author different material structures: cast/abraded mineral, painted steel,
   machined edges, rubber seals, glass and oxidized copper. Place dirt, abrasion
   and moisture where use, gravity and heat would produce them.
4. Sculpt natural formations with convincing fracture scale, stratification,
   varied silhouettes and integrated mineral veins. Thin glowing lines on a
   smooth mound alone cannot carry the material's identity.
5. Rebuild weapon, gloves and actors with convincing assembly/anatomy, grip,
   joint transitions and animated weight. These occupy the player's attention
   and currently remain far below the desired quality.
6. Compose light around focal objects, intermediate depths and readable routes.
   Inspect actual FPS and perspective orbital cameras, movement and sustained
   native performance. More bloom, darkness or texture noise cannot substitute
   for the missing construction and composition.

Use these principles in Forgeborne's original industrial world. Rob's visual
review, complete play loop and fun gate remain open; this study is not another
accepted art milestone.
